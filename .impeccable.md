## Design Context

### Users
StarPilot owners reviewing recorded drives through Galaxy on phones, over local access or a tunnel.

### Brand Personality
Clear, dependable, intuitive. Playback should inspire confidence through predictable behavior and accurate feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep Galaxy's existing interface. This review targets playback compatibility and useful loading and error states, with production quality as the bar.

### Design Principles
- Make playback work across supported phones and desktop browsers.
- Prefer the smallest complete fix and reuse the existing efficient media pipeline.
- Explain failures accurately and give users an actionable recovery path.
- Keep controls responsive and avoid unnecessary device work.
